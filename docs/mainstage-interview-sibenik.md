# Mainstage Interview — Whisper Voice Challenge

**Event:** Innovate Together Sibenik | October 13–14, 2026
**Speakers:** Anton Giertli (Solution Architect, SK/CZ) & Stas (Sales Manager, Poland)
**Duration:** ~2 minutes

---

**Stas:** Before we start, I've got Anton here — Solution Architect from our Slovak-Czech team. Anton, I heard you've been doing something unusual with AI. What's the story?

**Anton:** I've been working with a customer where a lot of their AI use cases came down to one thing — speech to text. And the catch was, none of it could go to the cloud. The data is just too sensitive. That conversation got me thinking — how good is open-source speech-to-text AI today? And can you run it fully on-premise?

**Stas:** And can you?

**Anton:** You can. There's a model called Whisper — you deploy it on OpenShift AI, on your own infrastructure, and it just works. And because I'm a playful person, I decided the best way to show it off is a game.

**Stas:** A game.

**Anton:** You walk up to the booth, the game gives you a challenge phrase, you speak it into the microphone, and Whisper transcribes your voice in real time. Then we score the accuracy.

**Stas:** Sounds simple enough.

**Anton:** Here's the thing — Whisper understands 99 languages. And I heard this is the most international Innovate Together yet — 21 countries in this room. So I need your help. Come to the booth and test it in your own language. Polish, Croatian, Hungarian, German — whatever you speak. Help me stress-test this AI. And if you beat the leaderboard — there are prizes.

**Stas:** So you want the audience to break your AI.

**Anton:** Exactly. Stop by the stand, play the game, and see what on-premise AI can do.

**Stas:** You heard him. Stop by, speak your language, break his AI. Anton, thanks.

**Anton:** Thanks, Stas.
